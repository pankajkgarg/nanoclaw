# Image & Animation Prompt Examples

These are EXAMPLES for inspiration — not a fixed set. Always craft custom prompts tailored to what the user actually wants. Combine elements, invent new scenes, draw on any culture, season, location, or mood.

## Example Hero Image Prompts

**Indian Temple:**
Interior of ancient Hindu temple mandapa at night. Stone carved pillars, intricate deity reliefs in alcoves. Floor covered in rose petals. Row of diyas on stone ledge, warm amber bokeh glow. Tabla drums, Bansuri flute, Sitar arranged in foreground. Incense smoke rising. Golden candlelight, sacred meditative atmosphere, cinematic. 8K photorealistic.

**Himalayan Mountains:**
Majestic Himalayan peaks at golden sunrise, snow-capped mountains, glacial valleys with morning mist. Prayer flags in foreground. Warm golden light on peaks, deep blue-purple shadows. Serene, cinematic landscape. 8K.

**Sacred River:**
River Ganges at golden sunrise, ancient stone ghats. Morning mist rising from calm water. Rose petals floating, small diyas on leaf boats. Temples in background, warm amber light. Spiritual atmosphere. Cinematic, 8K.

**Forest:**
Ancient Indian forest at golden sunrise, massive banyan trees, sunlight streaming through canopy creating god rays. Morning mist on forest floor. Wildflowers, moss-covered roots, birds. Peaceful, primordial. Cinematic, 8K.

**Krishna Devotional:**
Lord Krishna playing bansuri under Kadamba tree by Yamuna river at golden dusk. Peacocks, lotus in water. Divine golden-blue aura. Vrindavan garden, jasmine and marigolds. Sacred, devotional. Indian art meets photorealism. 8K.

**Winter Morning:**
Indian village at winter dawn, frost on grass, foggy path through mustard fields. Bare trees silhouetted against pale golden sunrise. Temple spire through mist. Peaceful, nostalgic. Cinematic, 8K.

**Japanese Zen Garden:**
Serene Japanese zen garden at twilight, stone lanterns casting soft golden light, raked gravel patterns, bamboo grove swaying. Koto instrument in foreground, cherry blossom petals. Mist over still water. Cinematic, 8K.

**Tibetan Monastery:**
Ancient Tibetan monastery at sunrise, prayer flags fluttering, Himalayan peaks with snow in background. Singing bowls and prayer bells on stone altar, butter candles glowing. Sacred, meditative. Cinematic, 8K.

## Example Animation Prompts (for Kling)

Describe ONLY the subtle movement. Keep it cinemagraph-style — most of the scene stays still.

**Candle/temple scenes:**
incense smoke slowly spirals upward, candle flames flicker gently in soft draft, rose petals shift slightly, everything else remains completely still and serene

**Water/river scenes:**
water ripples gently on surface, morning mist slowly drifts across, leaves on water float subtly, light reflections shimmer, camera holds completely steady

**Nature/forest scenes:**
leaves sway imperceptibly in gentle breeze, sunlight dapples shift on forest floor, mist drifts softly between trees, camera holds steady

**Mountain/sky scenes:**
clouds drift slowly across peaks, prayer flags flutter gently, golden light gradually shifts on snow, everything else remains majestic and still

**Garden scenes:**
flower petals sway slightly in breeze, butterfly wings flutter, water surface ripples gently, morning dew glistens, camera steady
